Looking at the screens, there's a 
HomeScreen with a map and UI elements, 
SearchResults for selecting ride options,
 OrderScreen for tracking orders,
  UserTrack for real-time tracking,
   DestinationSearch for inputting locations, 
   SplashScreen for the loading screen,
    AuthLoadingScreen for role-based redirection, 
    RoleScreen for selecting user roles,
 and ProfileScreen for user profiles.

 The flow starts with the App component rendering 
 the AppNavigation within a Redux Provider.
  The navigation stack begins with the SplashScreen,
   then moves to AuthLoadingScreen which checks the user's role
    and navigates accordingly. If not authenticated,
     the user goes to RoleScreen to choose their role.
      Depending on the role, they're directed to registration 
      or login screens. After registration, users fill out 
      their profile in ProfileScreen,
  which then navigates them to their respective home screens.
